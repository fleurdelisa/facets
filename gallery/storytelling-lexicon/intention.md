# Intention
* a glossary of storytelling devices, tropes, and concepts (the underdog / dark horse, the journey home, etc.)
* covers every layer: master plots, emotional arcs, structures + their beats, character archetypes, tropes, devices, motifs
* entries link to each other: broader/narrower, opposites, related, "often confused with" (underdog vs dark horse), stages of a structure
* side-by-side view of story structures (hero's journey vs save the cat vs freytag), arcs drawn as little fortune-over-time lines
* glossary is public; definitions pulled from tv tropes (cc by-nc-sa, attributed) plus hand-written ones for booker / polti / vonnegut / beat sheets
* private catalog: when i'm watching a show, log what i spot (which entry, where, how it's played: straight, subverted, inverted, lampshaded...)
* each entry shows my sightings, each work shows everything i've tagged in it
* my own notes on entries, and custom entries for things tv tropes doesn't have
* quick-add from my phone in a few taps
* lives at apps/storytelling-lexicon, supabase for the database, hosted on fleurdelisa.dev
