# Chicken Congee

An old friend made this dish for me when I was sick. It was the most tender chicken I've ever had and introduced me to the technique of velveting.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 8 cups water
- 1 lb chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on)
- 2 inches fresh ginger, sliced
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 scallions, chopped
- Cilantro for garnish
- Soy sauce for serving
- Sesame oil for drizzling

## Instructions

1. Step 1: Wash rice: We'll start by washing our rice (1 rice cup) in a bowl:

- Fill the bowl with some water
- Massage and mix the rice around with your hands
- Drain the water
- My dad prefers to repeat this 3 times, for good measure

    It may seem counterintuitive to rinse rice that's just going to be cooked down in water, but this is an essential step! Washing the grains not only removes dirt, but also excess starch that can lead to gummy, sticky congee.

2. Step 2: Prepare the chicken: Season the chicken thighs with salt and let them sit while you prepare other ingredients:

- Pat the chicken dry with paper towels
- Season both sides generously with salt
- Set aside at room temperature for 10-15 minutes

3. Step 3: Start the congee: In a large pot, combine the washed rice and water:

- Add the rice to a large pot or Dutch oven
- Pour in 8 cups of water
- Add the sliced ginger
- Bring to a boil over high heat

4. Step 4: Add the chicken: Once the water is boiling, add the seasoned chicken:

- Carefully place the chicken thighs into the boiling water
- Reduce heat to medium-low
- Let it simmer, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking
- Cook for 45-60 minutes until the rice has broken down and the congee is creamy

5. Step 5: Shred the chicken: Remove the chicken from the pot and shred it:

- Use tongs to carefully remove the chicken pieces
- Let cool slightly, then remove bones and skin
- Shred the meat into bite-sized pieces
- Return the shredded chicken to the pot

6. Step 6: Season and serve: Taste and adjust seasoning, then serve hot:

- Add white pepper and additional salt if needed
- Stir in the vegetable oil for richness
- Ladle into bowls
- Top with chopped scallions, cilantro, a drizzle of soy sauce, and sesame oil

## Time

- Total: 1 hour 15 min

## Servings

4-6 servings
