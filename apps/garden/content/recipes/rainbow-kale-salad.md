# Rainbow Kale Salad with Sesame Miso Vinaigrette

My dear friend Wen and I made this together during one of our sleepovers! It was back when we were both still living in Fremont, merely 7 minutes away. It was the first time in my life that I lived so closely to a close friend and it felt like a childhood dream was unlocked.

## Ingredients

### Salad

- 1 bundle of curly kale, stems removed and cut into bite sized pieces
- 1 small beet, grated
- 1 small watermelon radish, thinly sliced
- 2 medium carrots, grated or shredded into ribbons
- 1/4 cup raw, unsalted pumpkin seeds
- 1/4 cup dried cranberries
- Avocado, cubed
- Olive oil
- Juice from half of a lemon
- 2 teaspoons chia seeds or sesame seeds
- Pinch of sea salt
- Black pepper

### Dressing

- 3 garlic cloves
- 2 tablespoons white miso paste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 3 teaspoons honey
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Sprinkle of cayenne

## Instructions

1. Step 1: Massage the kale: Place the chopped kale into a medium bowl and drizzle with lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of salt:

- Use your hands to massage the leaves until they wilt and soften
- The overall volume of kale should decrease dramatically

2. Step 2: Make the dressing: Add all the ingredients into a blender and combine until smooth:

- Be sure to taste the dressing and add any necessary adjustments
- I usually like to add more cayenne for a stronger kick; a spicy dressing for a spicy girl — I love that for me

3. Step 3: Kale, assemble!: This is the fun part, folks. Put your kale in a medium bowl or one that will allow you to toss the salad:

- Add the carrots, beets, radish, seeds, cranberries, and pepper
- If you're extra like me, you can arrange the ingredients in lines across the bowl to resemble the essence of a rainbow
- If you're a normal person who just wants to eat their salad, you can simply toss the ingredients in the bowl
- Generously drizzle the dressing on the salad and gently toss to combine
- Add the avocado and more dressing if needed, and give it one final toss
